Strategic Vision: Autonomous Product & Architecture Guardrail

What is this? In multi-agent projects, agents often propose ideas that break settled architecture (e.g., adding CGO, switching databases, or building out-of-scope features). Strategic Vision simulates proposals against your project's Non-Goals (what we refuse to build) and Durable Invariants (architectural tenets) before any code is written.

📖 How Strategic Vision Works Research Preference Model (RPM)
1. ⛔ Stored Non-Goals

Explicit negative constraints (e.g. "Pure-Go SQLite: CGO_ENABLED=0") stored in Sila knowledge base.

2. ⚖️ Autonomous Triage

Engine checks candidate proposals for negative keyword overlap, semantic conflicts, and active roadmap goals.

3. 🎯 Instant Verdict

Outputs a 0–100 Fit Score with 🟢 APPROVED, 🔴 REJECTED, or 🟡 NEEDS_ALIGNMENT rationale.
